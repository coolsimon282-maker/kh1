## What made things match (session 1)

- Script stack: `unk_174[3]` (0x180) is the stack top, `unk_174[top + 4]` (0x184 + top*4) is the top value.
  Push = `arg0->unk_174[3]++; arg0->unk_174[arg0->unk_174[3] + 4] = X;` (FM loads X after the increment,
  JP's versions load it first through a temp).
- `x ? 4 : 2` gives `li a0,2; li v0,4; movz v0,a0,x`. If the li order is swapped, swap the ternary

  the stack top in a register. If the target re-loads 0x180 after the global store, declare the global as an

  or a regswap. Adding `void f(s32);` fixed func_001CF940 and the JP "Regswap" nonmatch func_001DD620.

  A `s32* p = &...` temp gives swapped addu operands. (func_001DD288, func_001D5DC8)
